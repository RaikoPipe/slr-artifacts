AI assistance during human extraction. 

During the pilot extraction (§9), reviewers were allowed to use a general-purpose AI assistant. 
R1 used Claude Opus 5.5 and R2 used Qwen 3.8. T
hey used it to locate evidence in the full text, check provenance and page references, and phrase the _reasoning entries. 
Each reviewer chose every final value and is responsible for it. 
The assistants had no access to the extraction pipeline’s output, or to the other reviewer’s extraction. 
Because R1 and R2 used assistants from different model families, R1–R2 agreement measures two AI-assisted human coders, 
not unassisted humans. Agreement between humans and the LLM may also be inflated by the assistants.
